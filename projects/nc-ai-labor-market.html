EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS

AI Adoption & the North Carolina Labor Market

Analyzing how artificial intelligence exposure is associated with employment growth, wages, and changing skill demand across selected occupations and labor markets in North Carolina.

01 — PROBLEM DEFINITION

Research Question

How is increased AI exposure associated with employment growth, wages, and skill demand across selected occupations in North Carolina?

This project explores whether the widespread assumption that AI is eliminating jobs is actually visible in North Carolina labor-market data.
