ABOUT ME

Data, Technology & Business

I'm a data professional with more than eight years of experience working at the intersection of data analysis, business systems, and technology. Throughout my career, I've worked across a variety of industries and business functions, using data to solve complex problems, improve processes, and help stakeholders make better-informed decisions.

My experience has given me the opportunity to work throughout the data lifecycle from gathering and cleaning data to building datasets, developing reports and visualizations, supporting system implementations, and communicating findings to both technical and non-technical audiences. I've worked with technologies including Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Snowflake, SAP S/4HANA, SAP ECC, and Excel.

One of the things I enjoy most about working with data is moving beyond simply reporting what happened. I'm interested in understanding why patterns occur, determining what the data can reasonably tell us, and translating those findings into practical business decisions. That interest has also led me to continue developing my skills in statistics, programming, and data science at UNC Charlotte.

As I continue to grow professionally, my goal is to combine my background in business systems and analytics with more advanced statistical and data science techniques. I'm particularly interested in emerging technologies, automation, artificial intelligence, and the ways technology changes how organizations and people work.

Ultimately, I want my work to sit at the intersection of data, technology, and business decision-making—using technical tools not simply to produce analysis, but to solve meaningful real-world problems.
